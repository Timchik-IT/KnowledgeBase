[[Thresholding]]

### Global
- Single threshold for the entire image
	[[Global thresholding]]
### Local (or Adaptive)
- Threshold values are computed individually for different regions of the image

### Dynamic (Variable)
- Multiple thresholds are used to divide the image into more than two regions


### Hybrid
- Combines multiple thresholding approaches

### Edge-Based
- Thresholding is guided by edge detection (gradients) to segment objects from the background

### Statistical or Machine Learning-based
- Thresholds are learned or computed based on statistical models or machine learning algorithms
