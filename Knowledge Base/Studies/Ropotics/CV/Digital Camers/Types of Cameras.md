[[Digital Cameras]]

### Area Scan Cameras

- #### Capture the entire image at once (the whole area)
- #### Best for objects that are stationary or need high detail
- #### Applications: quality control, product inspection in static or slow-moving environments

### Line Scan Cameras

- #### Capture image line by line
- #### Best for continuous production lines
- #### Applications: conveyor belt inspections

### Time-of-Flight (ToF) Cameras

- #### Measure the distance to an object by calculating the time it takes light to reflect back
- #### Applications: 3D depth mapping
