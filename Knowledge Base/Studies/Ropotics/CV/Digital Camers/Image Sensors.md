[[Digital Cameras]]
#### Standard Cameras
- Monochrome and Color cameras
- Capture light intensity

#### Infrared (IR) Cameras
- Capture heat signatures, allowing detection of temperature differences

#### 3D Depth Sensors
- Measure depth information (spatial data) to create 3D models (e.g. point-clouds)

#### Specialized Imaging (X-Ray, UV)
- Analysis of internal structures, such as material inspection or medical imaging
