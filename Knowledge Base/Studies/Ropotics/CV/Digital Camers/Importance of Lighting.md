[[Digital Cameras]]
### Proper lighting is crucial to create clear images by highlighting features, reducing shadows, and enhancing contrast

### Lighting Techniques

#### **Backlighting**: illuminates from behind, highlighting the silhouette and edges of objects

#### **Brightfield**: illuminates the object from above, enhancing visible surface features

#### **Darkfield**: illuminates the object at a sharp angle, revealing surface scratches and
defects

#### **Ring and Dome**: provides even, shadow-free lighting across the object’s surface
