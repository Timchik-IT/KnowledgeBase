Получение изображений - [[Digital Cameras]]

#### Image acquisition is the process of capturing a visual representation of an object or scene using imaging devices

#### Produces a digital image that can be processed and analyzed for machine vision tasks

#### Components
- ###### Image sensor: converts light into electrical signals
- ###### Lens and aperture: control how light enters the camera, affecting the quality of the image
- ###### Exposure and shutter: control the duration that light hits the image sensor, impacting motion blur and brightness
