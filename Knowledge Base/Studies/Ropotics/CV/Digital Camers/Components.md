[[Digital Cameras]]

### Lens (lens systems)
- #### focuses light onto the image sensor
- #### multiple glass or plastic elements arranged in groups

![[Pasted image 20260909183226.png]]

### Aperture
- #### controls the amount of light reaching the sensor by adjusting the size of the lens opening

![[Pasted image 20260909183238.png]]


### Image Sensor

- #### converts light (light intensity) into electrical signals (voltage levels)
- #### consists of a grid of pixels* (with photodiodes)
- #### CCD (charge-coupled device) vs. CMOS (complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor)

![[Pasted image 20260909183345.png]]

### Shutter

- #### controls the exposure time by allowing light to hit the image sensor (each photodiode) for a specified duration
- #### faster shutter speeds reduce motion blur

![[Pasted image 20260909183453.png]]

### ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter)

- #### converts the analog electrical signals generated by the image sensor into digital signals

- #### produces the image in the raw format

### Sampling

- #### the analog signal is measured at regular intervals (sampling rate = resolution !!!)

### Quantization

- #### each sample is assigned a discrete value from finite set of values

- #### precision is determined by the bit depth (number of quantization levels)

### ISP (Image Signal Processing)

- #### **Demosaicing**: the raw data is converted into full Red-Green-Blue values

- #### **Noise Reduction**: reduces the noise from the signals

- #### **White Balance and Color Correction**: adjusts color balance to ensure accurate color based on lighting conditions

- #### **Sharpening and Contrast Adjustment**: enhances image detail and clarity

### Compression

- #### compresses the image to reduce the storage requirements (e.g., JPEG)
