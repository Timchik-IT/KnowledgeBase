[[Binary Images & Morphological Operations]]

- Converts a grayscale image into a binary image
- Segments regions of interest by setting intensity thresholds

![[Pasted image 20260916173806.png]]

Example of binary thresholding (left - grayscale, right - binary)

### Foreground
- Objects or regions of interest (1s or 255s - white)
- Focus of analysis (e.g., detecting shapes, edges, or objects)


### Background
- Everything that is not part of the objects or regions of interest (0s - black)
- Often ignored or treated as complementary to the foreground
